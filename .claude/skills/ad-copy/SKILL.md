# Approved copy — M&K Agency Inc, Agency R3001

Everything here has been checked against Allstate Agency Standards
(rev. July 15 2026, No. 43). Reuse rather than reinvent: new copy needs a new
compliance judgement, existing copy does not.

## Hard rules

Never write, in any language:

- Any price, premium, rate, discount amount, percentage, or "save money"
- Any comparison of carriers, or any carrier name other than Allstate
- "Independent" agent or agency
- Availability beyond **Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET** — no 24/7, no "always here", no "quote in 30 seconds"
- Any guarantee, approval, or assurance of coverage
- Scare framing ("don't gamble with your future")
- "Broker" — the agency is an Exclusive Agent

Say instead: licensed local agents, family-owned, three languages, a real person
answers, we explain what your policy covers, we stand with you at claim time.

## Callback wording

Every confirmation must name the window rather than say "shortly":

- EN — "A licensed agent will call you back during office hours — Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET."
- ES — "Un agente licenciado le llamará en horario de oficina — lun–vie, 9am–6pm ET."
- RU — "Лицензированный агент перезвонит в рабочие часы — Пн–Пт, 9:00–18:00 ET."

## Removed — do not reintroduce

`24/7 Customer Support` · `Compare Car Insurance Rates` · `We compare 15+ A-rated
Florida home insurance carriers` · `We Offer Competitive Rates And Personalized
Service` · `Independent insurance agency in Florida City` · `Bilingual, Real
People Who Answer` (the team is trilingual) · `Free quotes in 30 seconds` ·
`Don't Gamble with Your Financial Success` · `Cheap Full Coverage Insurance`

The last one was written by Google's asset generator, not by anyone at the
agency. If unfamiliar copy appears, check whether automated asset generation has
been switched back on before assuming a person wrote it.
